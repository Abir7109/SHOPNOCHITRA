How to Choose the Best Wedding Photographer in Dhaka

By Shopno Chitra Studio · October 19, 2026 · 7 min read

Searching for the best wedding photographer in Dhaka returns a hundred results and dozens of five-star pages. Ratings and follower counts help, but they are not the whole story. Here is the checklist we would use if we were the ones getting married.

The 10-point checklist

  1. Ask for full galleries, not highlight reels. Anyone can show 20 perfect photos. A full wedding gallery shows how the team handles difficult light, crowded stages and 10-hour days.
  2. Check consistency. Look at three different weddings on their page. The work should feel reliable, not like three different studios.
  3. Meet the actual team. Confirm who will shoot your event. At bigger studios the person on the portfolio is not always the person on your stage.
  4. Ask about backup gear. A wedding cannot be reshot. Professional teams carry backup bodies, multiple lenses and dual memory card slots.
  5. Read the delivery timeline. "Within 45 days" should be written in the contract, not promised verbally.
  6. Understand the editing style. Warm and candid, editorial, dark and cinematic — pick the style you love, because editing cannot be undone later.
  7. Ask what happens if someone falls ill. Reliable teams have trained members who can step in mid-event.
  8. Get pricing transparency. A good studio can explain exactly what is and is not included in every package.
  9. Check response time before you book. How fast they reply during the enquiry tells you how fast they will reply during your wedding week.
  10. Trust your gut in the meeting. You will spend more time with your photography team than almost anyone else on your wedding day. They should make you feel calm.

Red flags to take seriously

  • No full galleries available to view
  • No written contract or a contract without a delivery deadline
  • Prices that change between your chat and your quotation
  • Refusing to say who will actually photograph the event
  • Demanding 100% advance payment before the wedding (30–70% is standard in Bangladesh)

When should you book? (Peak season warning)

The Bangladeshi wedding season runs roughly from November to February, with a second window around April. During these months the best teams in Dhaka are fully booked two to three months in advance. If your dates fall in the peak season, start your search early and be ready to confirm quickly.

Frequently asked questions

When should I book my wedding photographer in Dhaka?

For peak season dates (November–February and April), book at least two to three months ahead. Off-season and weekday dates can often be booked within a few weeks.

How do I know if a wedding photographer is actually good?

Ask for two or three complete wedding galleries, not just best-of collections. Look for consistency across events, how they handle low light and large crowds, and whether the delivery matches what was promised.

Should I pay a 100% advance to book a photographer?

No. The standard practice in Bangladesh is a 30–70% advance to reserve your date, with the balance due near or on the first event day. Never pay in full before the event.
