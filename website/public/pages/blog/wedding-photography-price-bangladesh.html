Wedding Photography Price in Bangladesh: The Complete 2026 Guide

By Shopno Chitra Studio · October 19, 2026 · 8 min read

If you are planning a wedding in Dhaka or anywhere in Bangladesh, one of the most common questions is simple: how much does wedding photography cost? The honest answer is that prices range from around ৳15,000 for a small single-event booking to ৳2,00,000+ for multi-day coverage with a full photo and film team.

This guide breaks down real 2026 price ranges, explains what changes the number, and shows you how to compare quotes without getting burned by hidden costs.

One warning before you compare numbers: a cheap quote and an expensive quote are often not selling the same thing. A ৳25,000 package with one junior photographer covering four hours is a very different product from a ৳60,000 package with a two-person team covering your whole day.

What changes the price

  • Number of photographers. A bigger team covers more angles — the stage, the entry and the family at the same time.
  • Coverage hours. Most quotes cover 4–6 hours. Extra hours are usually charged per hour, so check the rate.
  • Photo only vs photo + film. Adding cinematography typically adds 40–60% to a photography booking.
  • Editing volume. Unlimited edited photos is not the same as 200 hand-retouched selects. Ask exactly what "edited" means.
  • Prints, albums and photobooks. Physical products are often priced separately.
  • Lead photographer. Many studios charge more when the founder or chief photographer personally shoots your event.
  • Drone, 4K and extra reels. Aerial coverage and social-media reels are common paid add-ons.
  • Travel and stay. Events outside Dhaka add travel, accommodation and meals for the team.

Hidden costs to check before you pay

  • Overtime per hour if your event runs late
  • RAW/unedited files charged separately
  • Album printing billed after delivery
  • Travel fees for outstation venues
  • Rush delivery fees (standard is often 4–8 weeks)
  • Drone permissions for restricted areas

Always ask for a written, itemised quote. The question that saves the most money is simply: "Is this all-inclusive, or are there any additional charges?"

How to get the best value

  • Book in the off-season or on less popular dates when you can — peak season (October to February) sells out early and premium pricing applies.
  • Bundle your events (holud + nikah + reception) into one booking instead of hiring separately for each day.
  • Decide on photo-only vs photo + video before you ask for quotes, so you are comparing the same thing.
  • Read full galleries, not just highlight reels. Consistency across a whole day is what you are buying.

Frequently asked questions

What is the average wedding photography price in Bangladesh in 2026?

Entry-level photography starts around ৳15,000 for a single event, mid-range professional packages run ৳40,000–৳90,000, and premium full-team, multi-day coverage goes from ৳90,000 to ৳2,00,000+.

Why do wedding photography quotes vary so much in Dhaka?

Team size, coverage hours, whether video is included, editing volume, prints and albums, the lead photographer, and travel all change the price. Two quotes with the same headline number can include completely different deliverables.

How much advance is needed to book a wedding photographer?

Most studios in Bangladesh confirm a date with a 30–70% advance, with the balance due before delivery or on the first event day. Always get the advance, inclusions and delivery timeline in writing.

How many photographers do I need for my wedding?

Small intimate events work well with one or two photographers. For weddings with 200+ guests, three or more team members let us cover the stage, the entry and the family at the same time.
