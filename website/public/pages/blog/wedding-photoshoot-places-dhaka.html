Best Wedding Photoshoot Places in Dhaka: 10 Locations We Love

By Shopno Chitra Studio · October 19, 2026 · 7 min read

Dhaka has more photographic gold than people give it credit for. Between the Mughal-era monuments, leafy parks and rooftop skylines, couples planning a pre-wedding or post-wedding photoshoot have plenty of options — the trick is knowing where, and when.

1. Lalbagh Fort

The most filmed backdrop in Dhaka: red sandstone walls, archways and the famous Diwan-i-Aam garden. Best in the early morning (7–9 AM) before the crowds arrive. Timeless, royal and instantly recognisable — every album deserves at least one frame here.

2. Ahsan Manzil (Pink Palace)

Pink walls, white colonnade, the Buriganga riverfront at your back. The light on the east face is gorgeous before 9 AM. Pair a red Benarasi sari with the pink backdrop for a colour combination that never fails.

3. Curzon Hall, University of Dhaka

Deep red brick, endless arches, high ceilings — arguably the most cinematic single building in the city. Best on weekday mornings; university grounds are busy during semester hours.

4. Ramna Park

The green heart of Dhaka. Canopy walkways, old trees and soft dappled light make it perfect for pastel outfits and romantic walking shots. Go at sunrise for empty paths.

5. Hatirjheel

Water, bridges and the city skyline. Sunset (5–6:30 PM) turns the lake gold; evening shots with city lights are a completely different mood. Weekday evenings are far less crowded.

6. Dhanmondi Lake

Soft reflections, wooden walkways and greenery on every bank. Great for a relaxed, natural look — and ideal for couples who feel shy in big public spots because there are quiet corners everywhere.

7. Bangladesh National Botanical Garden, Mirpur

Hundreds of species, flower beds and glasshouse greens. Tiny entry fee, huge variety of backdrops — one garden session here covers what five locations would elsewhere.

8. Baldha Garden, Wari

The oldest botanical garden in Dhaka: intimate, shaded and quietly beautiful. Slightly hidden in Old Dhaka, which means far fewer visitors — you can actually take your time.

9. Old Dhaka streets (Shankhari Bazar & Tanti Bazar)

For couples who want character: tin facades, rickshaws, lanterns and lived-in texture. Go early on a Friday for the calmest streets. The most "only in Dhaka" frames you will ever get.

10. Panam City, Sonargaon (day trip)

An hour outside the city: abandoned merchant mansions from the 1800s along a green canal. A perfect half-day shoot if you want something nobody else has. Budget travel time and a permit fee for entry.

Practical tips before you go

  • Timing beats location. Early morning (7–9 AM) and golden hour (4:30–6 PM) are the two magic windows. Midday light flattens everything.
  • Check permits. Some monuments charge a photography fee or require advance permission — your photographer should arrange this.
  • Match your outfits to the backdrop. Reds and golds pop against stone; pastels and whites glow in greenery; deep tones suit evening city lights.
  • Plan two locations maximum per session so you spend time shooting, not sitting in traffic.
  • Weekdays are your friend. A Tuesday morning at Lalbagh Fort feels like a private set.

Frequently asked questions

Which is the best time for a wedding photoshoot in Dhaka?

Early morning (7–9 AM) and golden hour (4:30–6 PM) give the softest, most flattering light. Avoid midday sun, and check weekend crowds at popular spots like Lalbagh Fort and Hatirjheel.

Do we need permission for photoshoots at Lalbagh Fort or Ahsan Manzil?

Yes, historic sites charge a photography or videography fee, and some restricted areas need advance permission. Professional teams usually arrange this for you as part of the shoot.

How many locations can we cover in one photoshoot?

We recommend a maximum of two locations per session. Travel time in Dhaka traffic eats into shooting time, and quality drops when you rush between too many places.
