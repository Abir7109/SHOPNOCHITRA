Pre-Wedding & Post-Wedding Shoots in Bangladesh: Ideas, Outfits & Prices

By Shopno Chitra Studio · October 19, 2026 · 6 min read

The wedding day itself is beautiful — but it is also fast, crowded and full of duties. That is exactly why pre-wedding and post-wedding shoots have become one of the most requested add-ons for Bangladeshi couples: one calm morning or afternoon, just the two of you, with no guests, no schedule and no rush.

Pre-wedding vs post-wedding: what's the difference?

  • Pre-wedding shoot: happens weeks before the wedding. You are rested, excited and free to spend 2–4 hours at a location of your choice. Perfect for save-the-date announcements and social media reveals.
  • Post-wedding shoot: happens after the ceremony — sometimes the same week, sometimes on an anniversary. You are relaxed in a way only married couples are, and many couples say these photos feel the most "them".

Where to shoot in and around Dhaka

Lalbagh Fort, Ahsan Manzil, Hatirjheel at sunset, the Botanical Garden, and old Dhaka streets are our most requested locations. We keep a full guide with timing and permit tips — see our list of the best wedding photoshoot places in Dhaka.

Outfit ideas

  • Pre-wedding, traditional: light pink, mint or pastel blue saris/shirts against green backdrops
  • Pre-wedding, modern: white-on-white or denim-and-white for a clean, magazine look
  • Post-wedding: your holud colours (yellow/orange) if you want a second chance at those photos, or red-and-gold for a regal contrast
  • Always: one coordinated accent detail — a dupatta, jewellery or pocket square — that ties both outfits together in the frame

What it costs in Bangladesh (2026)

As a standalone booking, a pre-wedding session with one photographer and one videographer usually runs ৳15,000 – ৳40,000 depending on duration, team size and location. Booked as an add-on to an existing wedding package, most studios (including us) offer it at a discounted rate — our standard add-on starts around ৳20,000 for a 1–2 hour session.

When should you schedule it?

  • Pre-wedding: 3–8 weeks before the wedding — after your outfits are final, before peak-season chaos begins
  • Post-wedding: within a month of the ceremony (the glow is real), or save it for a first-anniversary shoot
  • Golden hour sessions (4:30–6 PM) book out first — reserve your date early in wedding season

Both sessions can be added to any of our wedding photography collections or booked on their own. Book a call with your preferred month and we will send available slots and sample routes.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a pre-wedding photoshoot cost in Bangladesh?

Standalone pre-wedding shoots typically cost ৳15,000–৳40,000 depending on team size, duration and location. As an add-on to a wedding package, most studios offer a discounted rate of around ৳20,000 for 1–2 hours.

What should we wear for a pre-wedding shoot?

Choose outfits that photograph well against your location: pastels and light tones for greenery, reds and golds against stone monuments, and one coordinated accent detail between both outfits. Bring one change of clothes if your session is longer than two hours.

When is the best time to do a post-wedding photoshoot?

Ideally within a month of the wedding, while the celebration is still fresh and outfits are intact. Alternatively, a first-anniversary shoot is a lovely tradition — and the light in November–February is the best of the year.
