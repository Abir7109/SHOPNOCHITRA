Wedding Photography Packages in Bangladesh: What Is Actually Included

By Shopno Chitra Studio · October 19, 2026 · 7 min read

Bangladeshi weddings are rarely a single event. A typical celebration runs across a gaye holud, a nikah or wedding day, and often a reception — sometimes on both sides of the family. That is why wedding photography packages in Bangladesh are usually built around events, not just hours.

Photography-only vs photo + video combo

Most studios offer two tracks: photography-only packages and combo packages that add cinematography. A combo usually costs 40–60% more than photography alone, but booking both together almost always beats hiring two separate teams.

What every good package should include

  • The number of photographers and cinematographers, and who leads the team
  • Coverage hours per event — and the overtime rate
  • How many edited photographs you receive (and what "edited" means)
  • Film length if video is included — trailer vs full film
  • Prints and album or photobook details
  • Delivery format and timeline (pen drive, Google Drive, online gallery)
  • Travel terms for events outside Dhaka

Add-ons worth paying for

  • Instagram reels. A 60–90 second vertical edit is the fastest way to share the day with people who could not attend.
  • Drone coverage. Beautiful for outdoor venues and grand entries — check the venue's rules first.
  • Fine-art photobook. Prints survive hard drives. A designed album is what your family will actually hold for decades.
  • Express delivery. Worth it if you want photos for a reception slideshow or an early post-wedding gathering.
  • Pre-wedding shoot. Usually 1–2 hours at one location — great practice before the main event.

Questions to ask before booking

  1. Can I see two or three full galleries from weddings like mine?
  2. Who exactly will shoot my event, and have I seen their work?
  3. What happens if the event runs an hour late?
  4. How and when are the photos delivered?
  5. What is the cancellation and rescheduling policy?

Frequently asked questions

How many events should my package cover?

Most Bangladeshi weddings include a gaye holud, a nikah or wedding day, and often a reception. If you know all your dates before booking, packaging them together is simpler and usually cheaper than booking events one by one.

Is a combo package (photo + video) better than hiring separately?

Combo packages are usually 10–20% cheaper than two separate bookings, and more importantly the photo and film teams coordinate with each other, which shows in the final result.

What is typically not included in a wedding photography package?

Travel outside your city, drone permissions, additional printed albums beyond the package, and rush delivery are commonly excluded. Get every extra listed in the written quotation.
