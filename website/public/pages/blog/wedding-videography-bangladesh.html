Wedding Videography in Bangladesh: Films, Trailers & Reels Explained

By Shopno Chitra Studio · October 19, 2026 · 7 min read

Photos hold the day. Video holds the feeling — your mother's voice during the blessing, the dhol at the entry, your friends' laughter during the holud. Choosing the right wedding videography team is about deciding what you want to relive, and in what form.

What you get: trailer, full film, and reels

  • Cinematic trailer (2–5 minutes): the highlight film — music-led, emotional, and the version you will actually rewatch and share.
  • Full film (20–45 minutes): the complete story of the event, from getting ready to the farewell, with the key moments and speeches intact.
  • Instagram reels (60–90 seconds): vertical, fast and made for sharing — increasingly the most-viewed part of a wedding package.

Sound is more important than 4K

Here is the advice nobody gives: a beautifully shot film with bad audio is unwatchable, and a simply shot film with clean audio will move people to tears. Make sure your videographer uses lapel microphones for vows and speeches, and captures ambient sound for the entry and celebrations. Ask about their audio setup before you ask about resolution.

4K, Full HD and drone coverage

  • Full HD (1080p) is more than enough for social sharing and normal viewing. Most 2026 packages deliver 4K because the cameras shoot it natively — the practical difference on a phone screen is small.
  • Drone coverage shines at outdoor venues, garden weddings and grand entries. Inside Dhaka, restricted areas and venue rules may block flying — confirm before paying for it.

Questions to ask a wedding videographer

  1. How many cameras will be rolling — and where will they be placed?
  2. How do you capture vows and speeches
  3. What is the revision process if we want changes?
  4. Could I see a full 20-minute film, not just a trailer?

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between wedding videography and cinematography?

Videography traditionally means documenting the event; cinematic videography means the footage is planned, lit and edited like a short film with a trailer, colour grading and music. Most premium packages in Bangladesh today are cinematic.

Is drone footage included in wedding videography packages?

Drone is usually a paid add-on (৳15,000–৳25,000). It works best at outdoor venues; indoor and restricted areas may not permit flying, so confirm with your venue and videographer first.

How long does it take to deliver the wedding film?

A trailer is usually ready within 1–2 weeks, and the full film within 4–8 weeks depending on the season. Get the delivery timeline written into your contract.
