Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Shopno Chitra ("we", "us", "our") is a wedding photography studio based in Mohammadpur, Dhaka, Bangladesh. This policy explains what personal information we collect through our website and our photography services, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you submit the "Book a call" or contact form, or reach us by email, phone or social media, we collect the details you provide — typically your name, email address, phone number, event date and location, and any message you send.
  • Information collected automatically. When you browse the site we may collect basic technical data such as your device type, browser, approximate location, and the pages you visit. We also record campaign parameters (for example UTM tags) that describe how you reached our site.
  • Cookies and similar technologies. We use a small number of cookies and local storage entries that are necessary for the site to function and to remember your cookie choice. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange a call or consultation.
  • To prepare quotations, confirm bookings and deliver our photography services.
  • To send service-related messages about your booking.
  • To understand how the website is used so we can improve it.
  • To meet legal, accounting and tax obligations.

3. Legal basis and consent

We process your information on the basis of your consent (which you give when you submit a form or contact us), the performance of a contract with you (your booking), and our legitimate interest in operating and improving our studio. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us.

4. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that help us operate, such as our web host and email/communication tools, under appropriate confidentiality obligations.
  • Professional advisers (for example accountants) where required.
  • Authorities where we are legally required to do so.

Our site contains links to third-party platforms such as Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp. Their handling of your information is governed by their own privacy policies.

5. Retention

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and, if you become a client, for the duration of our relationship and a reasonable period afterwards so we can serve you and meet legal requirements. You can ask us to delete your information at any time, subject to any records we must keep by law.

6. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, update or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to stop using it for marketing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please share sensitive details only through the channels we provide.

8. Children

Our services are intended for adults. If a child's information is shared with us (for example, as part of a family session), it is provided by a parent or guardian, and we treat it with the same care described in this policy.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last revised. Material changes will be announced on this page.
