Straight takeaway: your photos already look like a premium brand. your online presence doesn't match them yet. The website's job is to close that gap.

One rule we'll hold all the way through: the interface stays quiet — ivory, black, thin gold lines — so the red comes from your photographs. If the UI shouts, the photos stop speaking.

✓ Typography Direction

  • Headlines: a bold, high-contrast display serif (Playfair Display / Cormorant) in heavy weight — matches the gold, ornate, bridal feel.
  • Body: a clean sans-serif (Inter / Source Sans) so packages and pricing are effortless to scan on a phone.
  • Bengali support built in — স্বপ্নচিত্র and any Bangla copy must render properly, not fall back to a system font. Your audience searches in Bangla too.

03What the Website Will Have — and Why

Every feature below is here for one reason: it moves a curious visitor one step closer to booking. Nothing decorative for its own sake.

FeatureWhat it isHow it pulls in clients
1. Full-screen Hero Your cinematic cover shot, full width, with "Wedding Photography & Cinematography — Dhaka" stated plainly, and one big button: Check Your Date. A couple landing here knows in 3 seconds: who you are, what you do, where you are. Vague studios lose the click; clear ones keep it.
2. Portfolio by Story Not a junk drawer of 500 images — complete wedding albums, one per couple, browsable like a magazine. Red/walima sets, holud, outdoor, candid. Couples buy from full stories, not single hero shots. Seeing a whole day handled beautifully answers "can they handle my wedding?" without a single phone call.
3. Packages & Starting Price 3 clear tiers (e.g. Photography / Photography + Film / Premium with drone & FPV), each listing hours, edited photos, album, delivery time. A visible starting price. This is the #1 conversion feature in our industry research. People who can't afford you self-select out; serious couples see you're professional and inquire faster. Silence on price = fewer leads.
4. Real Testimonials Named couples, wedding date, their photo from the album, pulled from your Facebook comments and reviews. You have 53K followers but only 4 public reviews — that's proof sitting unused. On the site, every claim gets backed by a real face. Trust is what turns a browser into a booking.
5. Date-Check Form Short and sharp: wedding date, venue, phone/WhatsApp, package interest. Four fields. An automatic "we'll reply within 24 hours" message. Asks exactly the questions you'd ask anyway, so your first reply can be complete and professional. Fewer fields = more submissions. Every inquiry lands structured, in order, first-come first-served.
6. WhatsApp & Click-to-Call Sticky button on mobile, your 01830 number one tap away. In Bangladesh, deals happen on WhatsApp. This removes the last inch of friction — especially for the 80%+ of visitors arriving from Facebook on a phone at night.
7. Live Instagram + Facebook Feed Your latest posts embedded on the site, auto-updating. The site never looks stale, you maintain nothing extra, and visitors who found you on social can keep following you there. Both channels feed each other.
8. Film Showreel Section A 15–30 second highlight reel of your cinematography + drone footage, autoplay-muted with a fallback image. Video is your premium upsell. A single reel sells cinematography packages better than any paragraph — and drone/FPV is something most local competitors can't offer.
9. About & Team Your story, your gear, your team, your studio address in Mohammadpur. Second-most-visited page on any studio site. Couples are choosing people, not pixels — a real face behind the camera is a competitive advantage.
10. SEO & Google Presence Pages that actually say "wedding photographer in Dhaka / Mohammadpur", fast loading, mobile-first, connected to Google Business Profile. Facebook reach is rented — the algorithm decides who sees you. A website is an asset that brings search traffic every month, forever, at no per-lead cost.

04How This Brings You More Clients

This is the part that matters, so we'll be blunt about it.

53KFollowers you can redirect
24/7Working hours, no staff needed
0Cost per lead, unlike ads

Right now, interest leaks out of your business at three points. The website plugs all three:

Leak 1 — The dead website link. Every week, someone taps "website" on your Facebook page and gets nothing. Some of them were ready to book. You'll never know how many. The new site turns that dead end into your best salesperson.
Leak 2 — No place to show full work. Facebook scrolls past. Albums are buried. A couple who loved one photo can't find the rest, can't see your packages, can't judge your price range — so they DM and wait, or they don't. On the site, the whole decision happens in one sitting, then they contact you already convinced.
Leak 3 — Proof is invisible. 53,000 people follow you, yet a stranger sees "4 reviews, no rating." On the website, testimonials with real couples' names and photos sit next to every package. That's the difference between "looks nice" and "safe to pay a deposit to."

✓ Where the new clients actually come from

  • Your own audience, finally monetized. 53K followers currently give you reach and nothing else. The link in your bio becomes a booking funnel that works while you sleep.
  • Google search you don't pay for. Couples Google "wedding photographer Dhaka" every day. Today that leads to your competitors with working sites. After launch, it can lead to you — once, and again next month, at no cost per click.
  • Referrals that land somewhere professional. When a happy client tells a friend, the friend checks you out within minutes. A fast, gorgeous site with visible packages closes that referral. A dead link kills it.
  • Higher-value bookings, not just more inquiries. Published packages and a starting price attract couples already in your budget range — fewer wasted calls, more inquiries that end in signed contracts.
  • It works while you're shooting. Weddings keep you offline for days. The site keeps answering questions, showing work, and collecting dates and venues from couples who'd never have waited for a reply.
  • Something your competitors in Dhaka already have. Studios half your size now run booking-ready sites with package builders and working forms. Your work is better than theirs — the site makes sure people know it.
The one-sentence version: you've already done the hard part — you built the skill and the 53K audience. The website is simply the missing piece that converts attention into signed bookings instead of likes.
